Move-in and move-out cleaning FAQs

What is the difference between move-in and move-out cleaning?

Move-out cleaning prepares a home you are leaving for a final walkthrough, handoff or closing. Move-in cleaning gets a new home truly fresh before your furniture arrives. Both are detailed cleans of an empty property.

When should I book move-out cleaning?

After the movers have finished and the home is empty, and before your final walkthrough, key handoff or closing. Book as soon as you have a date.

Do you work with realtors, landlords and property managers?

Yes. We can coordinate access around showings, lockboxes and turnover dates. Call (843) 707-2268 to discuss recurring turnover work.

Does the home need to be empty?

For the best results, yes. Remove belongings and trash first so the team can reach every surface, cabinet and floor.
